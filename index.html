Good research habits

  • Start with the author, title, publisher and publication date; check whether the author is a recognized scholar or primary witness.
  • Distinguish primary sources from secondary sources. A treaty, letter or official record is not the same as a later historian's interpretation.
  • Compare accounts from more than one author and consider the source's perspective, purpose and historical context.
  • Record page numbers and full bibliographic details as you read.
  • Respect copyright. Use official download or lending options; do not assume every scanned book is free to redistribute.
